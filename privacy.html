Privacy Policy

Effective 27 July 2026

Utilize helps you build and maintain a daily routine to prevent and recover from burnout. Here's what data that takes, why, and what rights you have over it.

1. Data we collect

Account data: your email and password (handled by our backend provider, Supabase, using industry-standard hashing — we never see it in plain text).

Onboarding and profile data: your stated persona, priorities, wake/sleep times, work hours, commute, weekly screen-time estimate, goals, upcoming-event details, and your self-reported burnout level.

Usage data: your generated daily schedule and which tasks you complete, your streak, and a timestamped history of every burnout check-in you make.

Subscription data: whether you have an active Premium subscription. Payment itself is handled by the App Store/Google Play and Superwall — we never see your card details.

2. Why we collect it

To build and personalize your daily schedule (including adjusting it based on what you actually complete), show your burnout trend, keep your account in sync across devices, verify your subscription, and send an optional event-countdown reminder.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

3. Sensitive data

Your burnout level and check-in history may count as sensitive personal data in some places. We collect it only when you choose to check in, use it solely to personalize your own schedule, and never use it for automated decisions about you.

4. Who we share data with

Supabase (account + database hosting), Superwall (subscription/paywall), Apple App Store / Google Play (payments, sign-in), and Expo (notification delivery). None of these use your data for their own advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it — profile, schedule history, burnout check-ins — at any time from Settings → Delete account. This is immediate and irreversible.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Delete your account yourself in-app, or email us for anything else.

7. Children's privacy

Utilize is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date above whenever this policy materially changes.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: enquiries@utilizeapp.org
